Our Story

A Little Place to Slow Down.

Brew & Bloom Café is a fictional demo café created to show how a local café in Indore could present its menu, atmosphere, location and contact details online — from a first glance at the homepage to a table reservation.

Every section here — the menu, the gallery, the reservation form — is built to demonstrate what a real, working café website can look and feel like.

This is a demo project built for a web-design portfolio. Brew & Bloom Café is not a real business.
